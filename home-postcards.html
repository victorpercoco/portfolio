Victor Percoco

Home postcards

An ongoing series of illustrated postcards paying tribute to the three cities that shaped my family: Isla de Margarita (Venezuela), Bari (Italy), and Montréal (Canada). Each composition begins as a hand-drawn illustration and custom lettering before being reconstructed digitally in Illustrator using geometric vector shapes and a limited colour palette.

Rather than pursuing realism, I distilled each city into a handful of recognizable landmarks, textures, and visual rhythms. The illustrations combine simplified forms, custom typography, and screenprint-inspired colour separation to evoke the feeling of place while remaining highly graphic.
